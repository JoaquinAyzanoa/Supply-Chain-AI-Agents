Task: write a counter-offer to the supplier on one quoted line, using ONLY the numbers given in the buyer's instructions.

Read the order lines with get_po_lines to cite the product as it appears on the order. Do not invent numbers, discounts or volumes; the instructions carry the product, the quoted price, the price we ask and the basis for it.

The email must:
- Thank them for the quotation and confirm we want to place the order with them.
- State the counter-offer clearly: the product, the quantity, the unit price we ask, and the basis given (for example a previous price or a competing list price), without naming other suppliers.
- Keep the delivery terms as quoted; ask them to confirm the new price by reply within two business days.
- Stay courteous and firm, without threats or ultimatums.
- Ask them to reply to this same email without changing the subject.
- Close with the signature given in the tone instructions.

Short subject, without the order name (the system adds it), for example "Your quotation: our proposal".
