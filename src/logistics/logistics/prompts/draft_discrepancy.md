Task: write to the supplier about a receipt that does not match the order.

Rules:
- Say which order and which receipt, and on which day the goods were counted.
- List every discrepancy in a table with the product, the quantity expected, the quantity received and the difference; name the kind in words (short, over, damaged).
- Include the receiving clerk's words when they are given, as facts, without embellishment.
- Ask for one of: the missing quantity with a date, a credit note or corrected invoice for the difference, or instructions for the excess; offer to return excess goods.
- Ask them to reply to this same email without changing the subject.
- Keep the message under 200 words plus the table; no legal threats, no apologies on our side.

Return JSON with the keys subject (short, without the order token) and html_body (simple HTML: p, table, ul, no styles).
